A new look for the page a player sees after their invite, and an optional banner.

- **Boxed sections.** A greeting, then Join, Your first time in and The map, each in its own box. Phones get one column. From 52rem wide the first steps and the map sit side by side. Sign-out is a quiet text button at the bottom. Text is a little smaller.
- **A banner.** `page.banner` names a PNG or WebP in `plugins/WelcomeWagon/`, at most 512 KB. It's drawn full width at its natural ratio, up to 44rem, with a dotted rim under it. The server's name stays in the page as a hidden heading, and it's the picture's description. Empty means no banner, and then the page keeps the logo and the visible name. A banner that's too big or isn't a real image is left out, and the console says why.
- **Shorter default steps.** If you've set your own `page.steps`, nothing changes for you.

## Also in this release

- **Your own colors and font** under `page.theme`, for light and dark, or always one of them (`mode: dark`). Every color pair the page uses is checked against WCAG AA first; if one fails, the whole theme is left out and the console names the pair. The theme is served as `/theme.css` after the page's own stylesheet, so the strict CSP stays as it is.

## Installing

Drop the jar in over 0.6.1. Nothing to rename or move. The new `banner` line is in the shipped `config.yml`; add `banner: ""` under `page:` if you want to see it.

## Tested

Unit and HTTP tests, the bot suite on Paper 26.1.2, and screenshots at 320, 390 and 1280 px. Not tried: Paper 26.2 and 26.3 bot runs, a real phone, a screen reader.
